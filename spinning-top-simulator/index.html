Free Browser Spinning-Top Simulator & Emoji Battles

Build an emoji top, choose your launch and watch a short arena battle in your browser. Emoji Blade is a free, independent spinning-top game: no account or game installation is required.

Play the spinning-top arena

Try a controlled launch experiment

For a repeatable setup, open Diamond versus Lightning in local mode. Set both tops to 85% power, Player 1 to -35 degrees and Player 2 to -145 degrees. Repeat a few rounds, then reduce only Player 1's power to 50%. Record the settings, result and battle time. Random critical clashes mean one round cannot establish a winning build.

For the full setup sequence, use the shared-device duel guide. To understand what the sliders change, read the launch and physics guide.

Looking for a Beyblade-style browser game?

If you enjoy launching spinning tops into a stadium, Emoji Blade offers a lightweight digital alternative. It is not an official Beyblade game and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Beyblade or its rights holders. It does not reproduce branded toys, parts or tournament rules.

Make a custom emoji top

Enter an emoji or character in the player field. Recognized characters have fixed ratings; other supported characters receive repeatable ratings generated from their input. Choose an armor look, then adjust launch power and angle. Armor is visual only: this is an emoji creator, not a customizable toy-parts assembly tool.

Try Diamond for high mass and endurance, Lightning for a high speed rating, or Fire for a high attack rating. These links select your top; you still choose the launch yourself.

What does the physics simulate?

  • Mass: affects collision response and collision energy loss.
  • Attack: contributes to collision damage.
  • Speed: affects initial movement velocity and the spin animation.
  • Endurance: reduces passive energy drain over time.

The arena uses simplified two-dimensional circular collisions, wall rebounds and energy depletion. The artwork looks dimensional, but this is not a full 3D angular-momentum simulation. There are no elemental counters or ring-out wins. A top loses when its energy is depleted.

Solo progression, custom rivals and local two-player battles

Single-player progression gives you a sequence of opponents, including Boss encounters. Select a custom rival to experiment without progressing your streak. In 2P Local mode, two people share one device and set their launches in turn before the battle begins. Online matchmaking is not available.

How to start on desktop or mobile

  1. Open the arena and choose your emoji.
  2. Pick your mode and rival.
  3. Set power and angle with the sliders, or drag backward from your top and release.
  4. Watch the result and change one launch setting for your next attempt.

The game supports mouse and touch input. Performance and browser support vary by device; there is no dedicated low-power mode. Read the controls guide for launch details or the FAQ for Boss rules, critical clashes and saved progress.

Compare builds without invented win rates

Character ratings explain useful trade-offs, not guaranteed winners. Launch conditions and random critical clashes affect each battle. Visit the character library to compare published ratings and use the tier list as a starting point, not a measured win-rate table.

Create a custom emoji top step by step

Open the arena, enter an emoji in the player input, then check its displayed ratings. Known presets keep their fixed stats; other normalized inputs receive deterministic generated stats. Set power and angle and launch. To compare two chosen inputs, use local 2-player mode and set both before Player 1 confirms.

What custom means here

Custom input is not a parts editor or free stat tuning. You cannot separately set mass, attack, speed and endurance. The same normalized input gives the same ratings in this game version, although launch conditions and random collisions still produce different outcomes. No account, hidden S-tier unlock or elemental ability is required.
