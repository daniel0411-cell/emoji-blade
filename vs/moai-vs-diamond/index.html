Maximum mass and endurance showdown

Moai reaches maxed 100 Mass and 100 Endurance. Diamond Core counters with 96 Mass, 98 Endurance, higher attack (72 vs 68), and greater speed (65 vs 52). This is a heavyweight duel of attrition where neither top suffers severe knockback.

Launch experiments

Because both tops absorb recoil effectively, battles frequently come down to spin stamina in the arena center bowl. Test launch angles to see whether Diamond's extra speed allows it to dictate early collisions before Moai's superior endurance takes control.

What these comparisons can tell you

Ratings describe trade-offs, not measured win rates. There are no elemental counters or shape-specific damage. Standard arenas rebound tops at the wall; optional Hazard arenas allow ring-out wins. Ordinary critical clashes multiply damage to both tops by 1.5; Xtreme Smash multiplies it by 1.8. Launch settings and random critical clashes can change the outcome.

Try a repeatable local test

The button selects both tops in local 2-player mode; it does not start an automatic battle. Player 1 sets power and angle, then Player 2 sets theirs. Begin with both at 75% power, record both angles and the outcome, then change only one setting. Repeat rounds because critical hits are random.
