Fast launch versus heavy endurance

Lightning has speed 99 and attack 88, compared with Moai at 52 and 68. Moai has mass 100 and endurance 100, compared with Lightning at 60 and 74. Speed affects launch movement, not an independent guaranteed damage bonus; Moai is not immune to collisions.

Launch experiments

Try Lightning with different contact angles rather than assuming maximum power is best. Test Moai with fewer damaging contacts. Moai has lower passive drain, but actual collision frequency and launch positions still matter.

What these comparisons can tell you

Ratings describe trade-offs, not measured win rates. There are no elemental counters, ring-out wins or shape-specific damage. Launch settings and random critical clashes can change the outcome.

Try a repeatable local test

The button selects both tops in local 2-player mode; it does not start an automatic battle. Player 1 sets power and angle, then Player 2 sets theirs. Begin with both at 70% power, record both angles and the outcome, then change only one setting. Repeat rounds because critical hits are random. Local tests do not advance stages or streaks; this starting setup is not an optimized winning build.
