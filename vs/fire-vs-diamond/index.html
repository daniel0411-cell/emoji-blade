Attack and speed versus mass and endurance

Fire has attack 95 and speed 88; Diamond has attack 72 and speed 65. Diamond leads in mass (96 vs 76) and endurance (98 vs 68). This is a contrast in ratings, not fire damage versus diamond armor: neither character has a special elemental ability.

Read the mass, attack, speed and endurance guide to see how these ratings affect play.

Launch experiments

With Fire, test early contacts without repeatedly exhausting your own energy. With Diamond, compare lower-contact launches. Do not infer a fixed counter relationship from one round or from the preset tier labels.

Use the power and angle experiment to record both launches and change one input at a time.

Can early contact offset the passive cost?

At equal 70% power, Fire starts at 8.624 and Diamond at 6.370 internal units/step. Fire loses 6.560 passive energy per 100 steps versus Diamond’s 4.910. Fire’s 95 Attack exceeds Diamond’s 72, but its lower Mass also raises its received-damage factor under otherwise equal conditions. Track both tops, not only damage dealt.

Formula examples at equal launch power

Passive drain per physics step is 0.048 + (100 - endurance) × 0.00055. Initial movement speed is speed / 100 × 14 × power / 100.

What these comparisons can tell you

Ratings describe trade-offs, not measured win rates. There are no elemental counters or shape-specific damage. Standard walls rebound tops. In Hazard mode, a top can lose by exiting a side pocket at sufficient speed, during Dash, or during Xtreme acceleration; slow pocket contacts rebound. On a closing collision, Dash, a 28% random check, or relative speed above 7.5 simulation units can trigger an ordinary critical for 1.5x damage to both tops. An active Xtreme rail boost makes it 1.8x. These multipliers affect damage, not collision impulse.

Set up a fair local comparison

Choose the standard arena. Set Player 1 to 70% power and -35°, then Player 2 to 70% and -145°. Start without Dash, repeat rounds, then swap the tops between left and right and mirror the angles. Restore the starting setup before changing only Player 1 power to 85%. These are experiment settings, not an optimized build.

Record the game version, both tops and sides, arena, power, angles, Dash use, rail contacts, critical hits, remaining energy and outcome. Keep Hazard trials separate; local practice does not advance solo stages or streaks. Full launch experiment guide.
