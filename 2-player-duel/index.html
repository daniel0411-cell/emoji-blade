2 Player Spinning-Top Battle Game - Local Emoji Blade Duel

Share one device, pick two emoji tops and take turns choosing your launches. Both tops enter the arena together after the second player confirms. No account or game installation is required.

Start a local two-player duel

How to play with two players on one computer

  1. Choose both tops. The button above opens 2P Local mode with a custom rival. The player emoji field sets Player 1; the custom rival field sets Player 2. Choose both before Player 1 confirms a launch.
  2. Player 1 sets the launch. Use power (30% to 100%) and angle (-180 to 180 degrees), then press the launch button. You can also drag backward from Player 1's top and release.
  3. Player 2 sets the launch. Pass the mouse or device to Player 2. Set the sliders or drag from the rival top, then confirm. Both launches are queued; neither top starts early.
  4. Watch the battle. The countdown begins after Player 2 confirms. The first top to run out of energy loses; simultaneous depletion is a draw.
  5. Rematch or change your setup. Select Play Again to return to the setup. During the countdown, use Cancel or Escape to reset both queued launches.

Local multiplayer, not online matchmaking

This is a same-device game with turn-based launch setup. It is not remote multiplayer, simultaneous keyboard control or a split-screen game. Mouse and touch input are supported, but actual touch behavior depends on the browser and device.

Already playing? Select 2P Local in the launch panel. Select a custom rival if you want to choose Player 2's emoji instead of using the current roster opponent.

Fair practice without changing your solo record

Local duels do not advance single-player stages, update the progression win streak or unlock progression trophies. Rival tops do not receive progression Boss multipliers in local mode. Base character ratings still differ, so choosing the same emoji for both players is useful for comparing launches.

Try Fire versus Fire with matching ratings, or try Lightning versus Diamond to explore speed and endurance trade-offs. There are no elemental counters or measured win-rate guarantees.

Can two people use a phone or tablet?

Both people can take turns on the same touch device. Choose both emoji before the first launch is queued, then hand over the device for Player 2's turn. On smaller screens, use the sliders if aiming directly in the arena is awkward. This page does not claim testing on every phone or tablet.

Why did my duel end differently this time?

Mass, attack, speed and endurance affect the simplified 2D simulation. Launch angle and power matter, and critical clashes include randomness. Cosmetic ring designs do not change collision geometry. Read the launch guide, compare the preset ratings, or check the two-player FAQ.

Prefer solo play? Open the arena in single-player mode. For an overview of the game, visit the spinning-top simulator page.
