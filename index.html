GAMEPLAY MANUAL

Mastering the Emoji Blade Arena

01

Choose & Customize Your Emoji

Type any Unicode emoji, letter or symbol into the input dock. The game algorithm computes its collision geometry, perimeter friction, and center of mass in real time.

02

Slingshot & Angle Mechanics

Use mouse drag or finger swipe directly in the arena to pull back the slingshot. Pulling further increases RPM launch speed and initial rotational torque.

03

Collisions, Recoil & Ring Outs

Blades lose spin energy during surface collisions and wall scrapes. Landing a sharp corner contact triggers a Critical Clash with 1.35x knockback, capable of ejecting rivals out of the stadium.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use any emoji from my mobile keyboard?

Yes! Simply open your mobile keyboard or copy-paste any emoji (such as 👾, 🦄, ⚽, 🐉, 🥑). The engine scans the glyph's pixel structure to generate custom physics.

What determines a Critical Clash?

A Critical Clash happens when your spinning tip collides with an opponent's flat flank at high relative velocity, dealing heavy spin stamina drain and screen-shaking recoil.

Is there an offline or mobile app version?

Emoji Blade is built as a lightweight Progressive Web App (PWA). You can tap "Add to Home Screen" in Safari or Chrome for a seamless, full-screen offline experience without app store downloads.
