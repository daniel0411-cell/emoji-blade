How to play Emoji Blade

Choose an emoji top, set launch angle and power, or pull backward from your top in the arena and release. Win when the rival runs out of energy first. Walls rebound the tops; there are no ring-out wins.

Stats and critical clashes

Mass affects collision response and damage, attack contributes to damage, speed affects launch velocity and the spin animation, and endurance reduces passive energy drain. Critical clashes apply 1.5x damage to both tops. Ring artwork does not change collision geometry.

Solo and local two-player modes

Progression includes a Boss every fifth stage. Local players choose launches in turn, then both tops launch together. Local duels do not affect solo streaks or trophies.
