How Custom Emoji Physics Work

Known presets use the game’s rating table; other inputs use a deterministic hash of the normalized character. The same input produces the same ratings in the current game version. These are game ratings, not measured grams or RPM. Tiers do not guarantee wins, and progression Boss bonuses are not included. Ratings can exceed 100.

  • Mass (Weight): Mass changes collision response and reduces received collision damage; it does not shorten hit-stop effects.
  • Attack (Clash): Attack contributes to energy damage dealt in top-to-top collisions, not damage from wall rebounds.
  • Speed (Rating): Speed affects launch velocity and visual spin speed; this is not a full angular-momentum simulation.
  • Endurance (Stamina): Endurance reduces passive energy drain, not damage from collisions.
