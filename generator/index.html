How Custom Emoji Physics Work

Emoji Blade utilizes a deterministic hash algorithm directly from the game engine. Every unique character or emoji produces a permanent, unchangeable stat profile across four core attributes:

  • Mass (Weight): Heavy tops absorb critical collision recoil and suffer less knockback when hit.
  • Attack (Clash): Contributes directly to the energy drain inflicted upon the rival top during wall rebounds and direct impacts.
  • Speed (Rating): Increases launch velocity, arena momentum, and visual spin speed.
  • Endurance (Stamina): Reduces continuous passive energy dissipation as your top spins in the center bowl.
