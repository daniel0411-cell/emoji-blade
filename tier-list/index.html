Emoji Blade Tier List: Best Emojis to Dominate the Arena

In Emoji Blade Online, not all characters spin equally. Every Unicode emoji inherits unique physical attributes—including mass density, attack rating, Speed rating, and spin stamina. Below is the updated competitive tier list to help you compare preset strengths; outcomes depend on launch settings and random critical hits.

The S-Tier (God Tier Clashers)

S-Tier tops boast near-perfect stat distributions or dominant physical traits that resist knockouts:

  • 💎 Diamond (Defense Core): Highest defensive mass density. Shrugs off heavy collisions and forces rivals to bounce off into walls. View Diamond Stats ↗
  • 🗿 Moai (Immovable Fortress): With 100 Mass, Moai is resistant to collision damage. Excels against light high-speed tops. View Moai Stats ↗
  • ⚡ Lightning (RPM Shredder): 99 Speed increases launch velocity. Critical hits use the same rule for every top. View Lightning Stats ↗
  • 👑 Crown (Royal All-Rounder): Symmetrical 86/88 balanced stats make it stable under all collision angles.

The A-Tier (Aggressive Strikers)

Tops designed for rapid knockouts through high-recoil collisions:

  • 🔥 Fire (Inferno Blaze): Devastating recoil momentum, great for corner traps. View Fire Stats ↗
  • 💣 Bomb (Kinetic Detonator): 98 Attack delivers immediate shock recoil upon direct impact. View Bomb Stats ↗
  • 🚀 Rocket (Linear Missile): High straight-line velocity, useful for high-speed opening collisions. View Rocket Stats ↗
  • 🥊 Boxing Glove (Brawler Strike): Heavy directional punch impulse.

Rock-Paper-Scissors Clash Dynamic

No single emoji wins 100% of the time. The arena physics follow a dynamic balance:

  • Heavy Armor (🗿, 💎) counters Light Speedsters (⚡, 🚀) by absorbing impact without recoil.
  • High Attack Strikers (🔥, 💣) counter Heavy Armor (💎) through concentrated critical strikes.
  • High Stamina Outspinners (🌪️, 🐢) counter Recoil Strikers (💣) by dodging initial bursts and winning the spin decay phase.
