PRESET TIER GUIDE

Emoji Blade Tier List: Preset Ratings & Stat Trade-Offs

In Emoji Blade Online, not all characters spin equally. Known presets use fixed mass, attack, speed and endurance ratings; other supported characters receive ratings derived from their input. Preset tiers are design labels, not measured win-rate rankings. Outcomes depend on launch settings and random critical hits.

The S-Tier Presets

S-Tier is a preset label, not a measured competitive ranking. These tops illustrate different stat trade-offs:

  • 💎 Diamond (Defense Core): 96 Mass reduces collision damage and 98 Endurance slows passive energy loss. View Diamond Stats ↗
  • 🗿 Moai (Immovable Fortress): With 100 Mass, Moai is resistant to collision damage. Its 52 Speed gives it lower initial launch velocity. View Moai Stats ↗
  • ⚡ Lightning (RPM Shredder): 99 Speed increases launch velocity. Critical hits use the same rule for every top. View Lightning Stats ↗
  • 👑 Crown (Royal All-Rounder): 86 Mass and Speed, with 88 Attack and Endurance, provide a balanced set of ratings.

The A-Tier (Aggressive Strikers)

These presets emphasize attack or launch speed, often at the cost of endurance:

  • 🔥 Fire (Inferno Blaze): 95 Attack and 88 Speed favor energetic launches; 68 Endurance means faster passive drain. View Fire Stats ↗
  • 💣 Bomb (Kinetic Detonator): 98 Attack contributes to collision damage; its 65 Endurance is lower than Diamond's. View Bomb Stats ↗
  • 🚀 Rocket (Linear Missile): High straight-line velocity, useful for high-speed opening collisions. View Rocket Stats ↗
  • 🥊 Boxing Glove (Brawler Strike): 84 Mass, 91 Attack, 82 Speed and 76 Endurance.

Stat Trade-Offs, Not Guaranteed Counters

There is no elemental or rock-paper-scissors counter system. Mass affects collision response and damage received, attack contributes to collision damage, speed affects launch velocity, and endurance reduces passive energy drain. Cosmetic armor does not change collision geometry.

Critical clashes can increase damage to both tops. Compare repeated launches instead of treating a single result as proof of superiority. See the complete 16-preset table and critical clash rules.
