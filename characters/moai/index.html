Frequently Asked Questions

Why is Moai Fortress considered the tankiest top in Emoji Blade?

Moai Fortress features maxed 100 Mass and 100 Endurance. High mass minimizes recoil knockback, while max endurance yields the slowest natural spin drain (0.048/step).

What is the best launch technique for Moai?

Because its 52 Speed is low, launch toward the center bowl and let aggressive attackers crash into your immovable defense and exhaust their energy.
