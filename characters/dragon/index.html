High attack with an endurance trade-off

Dragon has mass 92, attack 96, speed 90 and endurance 84. Compared with Crown, it is heavier and has higher attack and speed, but loses more energy passively. The dragon artwork does not grant elemental damage or a special ability.

Launch practice

Try Dragon against Diamond to compare contact-heavy and low-contact launches. Change angle before changing power, and monitor your own energy during critical clashes. High attack does not protect Dragon from incoming damage.

What these comparisons can tell you

Ratings describe trade-offs, not measured win rates. There are no elemental counters, ring-out wins or shape-specific damage. Launch settings and random critical clashes can change the outcome.
