Rocket vs Lightning: attack and mass for speed and endurance

Rocket has 92 Attack and 78 Mass, compared with Lightning at 88 and 60. Its 96 Speed is slightly below Lightning’s 99, and its 64 Endurance is below 74. Rocket therefore trades some launch speed and passive efficiency for higher attack and mass. The artwork supplies no thrust or piercing skill.

Set up a fair local comparison

Choose the standard arena. Set Player 1 to 70% power and -35°, then Player 2 to 70% and -145°. Start without Dash, repeat rounds, then swap the tops between left and right and mirror the angles. Restore the starting setup before changing only Player 1 power to 85%. These are experiment settings, not an optimized build.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Cosmic Rocket's main playstyle in Emoji Blade?

Cosmic Rocket relies on 96 Speed and 92 Attack to produce fast arena entries and rapid ricochets, attempting to land critical clashes before its 64 Endurance drains.

Does the pointed rocket shape change collision hitboxes?

No. All tops in Emoji Blade share a circular collision geometry. Cosmetic artwork does not alter hitboxes or grant directional piercing.
