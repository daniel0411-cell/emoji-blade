Vortex Tornado Top Breakdown

Vortex Tornado combines 94 Speed and 94 Endurance with only 65 Mass. It starts quickly and drains spin energy slowly, but low mass increases damage received from collisions. There is no stamina-steal ability: its advantage comes from the shared endurance formula.

Tornado vs Lightning: endurance without maximum speed

Tornado combines 94 Speed and 94 Endurance with 65 Mass and 78 Attack. Lightning launches faster at 99 Speed and has 88 Attack, but only 74 Endurance and 60 Mass. Tornado’s lower passive drain is a specific formula advantage, not evidence that it outspins most opponents.

Formula examples: what the ratings change

Frequently Asked Questions

How does Vortex Tornado perform in long matches?

With 94 Endurance, Vortex Tornado loses 5.130 passive energy over 100 physics steps, excluding contact and ability costs. Its 94 Speed gives a fast launch, but its 65 Mass increases the received-damage factor compared with heavier tops. These ratings do not establish a win rate.

Does Vortex Tornado have a vacuum or stamina-stealing ability?

No. There are no elemental or magical siphon mechanics. Its stamina advantage stems strictly from low passive decay under the game's endurance formula.
