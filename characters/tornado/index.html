Frequently Asked Questions

How does Vortex Tornado perform in long matches?

With 94 Speed and 94 Endurance, Vortex Tornado outspins most opponents in low-contact matches, though its 65 Mass makes heavy clashes risky.

Does Vortex Tornado have a vacuum or stamina-stealing ability?
