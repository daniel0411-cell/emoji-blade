Base Oni versus the stage 5 Boss

Oni has base mass 110, attack 100, speed 75 and endurance 90. In the first stage 5 progression encounter, Boss bonuses raise mass to 149 and attack to 125; speed and endurance stay unchanged. Selecting Oni in local or custom play uses base ratings, not that progression boost.

Launch practice

To prepare for the Boss, compare Diamond or Moai launches that reduce damaging contact. A local duel against base Oni helps you learn the controls but does not reproduce the boosted stage 5 encounter. No preset is a guaranteed Boss counter.

Read the stage 5 encounter separately

A selected Oni uses mass 110 and attack 100. The progression Boss uses 149 and 125, radius 44, and full launch power. A base practice round cannot establish a Boss win rate. Rehearse mirrored launch controls first; then watch the first heavy clash in progression.

Start a local trial at 70% power with Player 1 at -35° and Player 2 at -145°. Leave Dash unused, keep the canvas size fixed and change one setting at a time. These are trial inputs, not an optimal build. The link below selects base presets only.

What these comparisons can tell you

Ratings describe trade-offs, not measured win rates. There are no elemental counters or shape-specific damage. Standard walls rebound tops. In Hazard mode, a top can lose by exiting a side pocket at sufficient speed, during Dash, or during Xtreme acceleration; slow pocket contacts rebound. On a closing collision, Dash, a 28% random check, or relative speed above 7.5 simulation units can trigger an ordinary critical for 1.5x damage to both tops. An active Xtreme rail boost makes it 1.8x. These multipliers affect damage, not collision impulse.
