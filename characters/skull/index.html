Skull vs Fire: a small endurance trade-off

Skull has 94 Attack, 84 Speed, 74 Mass and 70 Endurance. Fire is slightly heavier (76), faster (88), and has slightly more Attack (95), but less Endurance (68). This makes the pair useful for investigating a small passive-drain difference alongside modest differences in the other three ratings.

Formula examples: what the ratings change

Passive drain per physics step is 0.048 + (100 - endurance) × 0.00055. Initial movement speed is speed / 100 × 14 × power / 100.

Frequently Asked Questions

What type of top is Grim Skull in Emoji Blade?

Grim Skull is an A-labelled preset with 94 Attack, 84 Speed, 74 Mass and 70 Endurance. Attack contributes to damage dealt, while incoming damage also depends on its own mass, relative speed and the opponent. These ratings are not a measured matchup result.

Does the skull graphic inflict special bleed or curse effects?

No. Collision damage is calculated strictly using physics formulas in game.js. Visual artwork does not add elemental effects or persistent bleed.
