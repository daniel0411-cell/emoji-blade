Pizza vs Diamond: quicker initial movement, less mass and endurance

Pizza has 78 Speed compared with Diamond’s 65, but lower Mass (74 vs 96), Attack (70 vs 72) and Endurance (86 vs 98). It offers a faster launch rather than a miniature version of Diamond’s defensive profile. All tops use circular collision geometry; the pizza artwork adds no shield.

Formula examples: what the ratings change

Passive drain per physics step is 0.048 + (100 - endurance) × 0.00055. Initial movement speed is speed / 100 × 14 × power / 100.

Set up a fair local comparison

Choose the standard arena. Set Player 1 to 70% power and -35°, then Player 2 to 70% and -145°. Start without Dash, repeat rounds, then swap the tops between left and right and mirror the angles. Restore the starting setup before changing only Player 1 power to 85%. These are experiment settings, not an optimized build.

Record the game version, both tops and sides, arena, power, angles, Dash use, rail contacts, critical hits, remaining energy and outcome. Keep Hazard trials separate; local practice does not advance solo stages or streaks. Full launch experiment guide.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Pizza Wheel's main combat profile in Emoji Blade?

Pizza Wheel trades high attack for 86 Endurance, 74 Mass, and 78 Speed, making it a reliable stamina top that outlasts glass-cannon attackers.

Does the pizza ring slice affect collision geometry?

No. The pizza slice artwork is cosmetic. All spinning tops use uniform circular collision geometry.
