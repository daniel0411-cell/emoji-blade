Balanced ratings, not a guaranteed champion

Crown combines mass 86, attack 88, speed 86 and endurance 88. It has no single maximum rating, making it useful for comparing launches without choosing an extreme speed or endurance preset. Its S tier is a preset label, not a measured ranking.

Launch practice

Compare Crown with Dragon: Dragon has higher mass, attack and speed, while Crown has higher endurance (88 vs 84). Start with the same power, record angles, then vary one setting. A higher total rating does not establish a win rate.

What these comparisons can tell you

Ratings describe trade-offs, not measured win rates. There are no elemental counters, ring-out wins or shape-specific damage. Launch settings and random critical clashes can change the outcome.
