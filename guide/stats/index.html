Mass, Attack, Speed & Endurance Explained

Updated November 13, 2026

Understand the four Emoji Blade ratings, compare real preset values and distinguish passive drain, collision damage, custom tops and Boss bonuses.

Four ratings, four different jobs

Ratings are game values, not measured grams or RPM. Mass affects collision response and reduces received collision damage. Attack contributes to damage dealt in top-to-top collisions. Speed affects launch movement and visual spin. Endurance reduces passive energy drain. Adding the four numbers does not produce a validated strength ranking.

Diamond trades launch speed and attack for greater mass and endurance. Lightning reverses that trade-off. Because all four values differ, this pair cannot isolate the effect of mass alone.

Endurance does not act as collision armor

Passive energy drain per 60 Hz simulation step is 0.048 + (100 - endurance) × 0.00055. Moai at 100 endurance loses 0.048 energy per step; Fire at 68 loses 0.0656. Collision losses, wall friction, Dash and rail costs are additional. This formula cannot predict a battle's total duration.

Attack and mass in a collision

Incoming collision damage includes the other top's attack, relative movement speed and your own mass. Higher mass reduces that received damage at equal other inputs. Criticals multiply damage to both tops by 1.5, or 1.8 during an active Xtreme boost. Endurance is not part of this collision damage calculation, and attack does not increase ordinary wall-bounce damage.

Worked collision: Moai versus Diamond

For one eligible closing collision, suppose the relative speed before the collision impulse is 5 simulation units. Moai has mass 100 and attack 68; Diamond has mass 96 and attack 72. Neither is a Boss. Both start this example with enough energy to show the entire loss.

Received energy loss = (relative speed × 1.8 + opponent attack ÷ 10) × (100 ÷ receiving mass) × clash multiplier × 0.35.

  1. Moai receives (5 × 1.8 + 72 ÷ 10) × (100 ÷ 100) × 0.35 = 5.670 energy at a 1× multiplier.
  2. Diamond receives (5 × 1.8 + 68 ÷ 10) × (100 ÷ 96) × 0.35 = 5.760 energy, rounded to three decimals.
  3. Apply the same clash multiplier to both losses, as shown below. These multipliers do not multiply the collision impulse.

This calculation excludes passive drain, walls, Dash and rail activation costs. Endurance does not enter this collision formula. Momentum response uses a separate calculation, and later paths can change the next collision: these numbers are not a duel prediction or a win rate.

Custom emoji and tiers

Known presets use fixed ratings; other normalized characters use a deterministic calculation. The same input produces the same base ratings in this version. Artwork and emoji theme do not add elemental counters or special collision shapes. Tier labels do not guarantee a win, and ratings can exceed 100: the base Oni preset has mass 110.

A Boss is more than its base preset

Progression Boss encounters multiply mass by 1.35 and attack by 1.25, rounded to integers. The first stage 5 Oni therefore has mass 149 and attack 125, rather than the base 110 and 100 shown in the calculator. Selecting Oni in a local duel gives its base preset, not those encounter bonuses.
