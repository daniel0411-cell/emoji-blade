How to Play

Boss Battles, Win Streaks & Saved Progress

2026-11-13

Learn the Oni and Invader Boss bonuses, what wins and losses change, which records are saved and how to return from practice to progression.

When do Bosses appear?

The solo progression roster repeats every ten stages. Oni appears at stages 5, 15 and so on; Invader appears at stages 10, 20 and so on. There is no direct link to skip to a Boss. Win earlier stages in progression to reach it.

Base stats versus Boss encounters

Boss encounters multiply base mass by 1.35 and attack by 1.25, rounded to integers. Oni becomes 149 mass and 125 attack, compared with base 110 and 100. Invader becomes 97 and 108, compared with base 72 and 86. Boss radius is 44 rather than 34. Selecting these characters in local two-player practice uses their base stats; the character tier label alone does not activate encounter bonuses.

What each result changes

In solo progression, a win advances one stage, adds one to the current streak and unlocks the winning player emoji badge if new. A loss resets the current streak to zero and keeps the same stage. A draw preserves both stage and streak. Local two-player, custom-rival and random-rival results do not advance these progression records.

What survives a reload?

Best streak and unlocked emoji badges are stored in this browser when local storage is available. The current stage and current streak start at 1 and 0 on a fresh page load. Reloading therefore does not resume a Boss attempt. Private browsing, clearing site data or blocked storage may remove or prevent saved records. There is no account-based transfer to another browser or device.

Practice without confusing the modes

Use a custom rival for base-stat practice; random-rival mode can select a Boss configuration. To resume progression after a local duel, select single-player AND the progression rival mode: switching to single-player alone retains the previous rival selection. A shared challenge link contains the characters, style and arena choice, but does not save the stage, streak, Boss bonus, launch power or angle.

A repeatable Boss attempt

Record your character, stage, arena, power, angle, Dash use and result. Keep the character and arena fixed, change one launch setting and repeat several attempts because critical collisions are random. Higher power is not a guaranteed solution, particularly near Hazard pockets. A base-stat Oni duel can help rehearse controls, but cannot establish a win rate against the larger boosted stage encounter.

Check the encounter before changing your launch

Boss mass and attack are rounded after multiplying the base ratings by 1.35 and 1.25. Speed and endurance stay at their base ratings. The larger radius changes contact geometry as well as the visible size.

Both Bosses launch at 100% power. Their initial angle varies from -165° up to, but not including, -125°. Repeating your own input therefore does not fix the enemy launch. Use the standard arena first if you want to separate energy loss from Hazard ring-outs.

Stage 5 Oni: diagnose the first heavy clash

  1. Reach stage 5 in solo progression. Choose a familiar top: Dragon offers mass 92 and attack 96; Crown trades some of those ratings for endurance 88. Neither is a verified counter.
  2. Try 70% power and -35° as a starting observation, with no Dash. Watch where the first collision happens and how much energy each top loses. Oni's 149 mass reduces its received collision damage; its 125 attack contributes to yours.
  3. If a large early energy drop follows a critical clash, retry with only the launch angle changed. If a rail boost precedes it, record that too. Increasing power changes both the path and collision speed, so it does not isolate attack.
  4. If you lose, use Retry to remain on the current stage. Keep the arena and character fixed for several attempts before comparing another preset.

Practice controls against base Oni separately. Its 110 mass, 100 attack and smaller radius do not reproduce this encounter.

Stage 10 Invader: separate launch speed from survival

  1. Reach stage 10 in progression. Invader retains speed 90 and launches at full power; do not assume its smaller mass than Oni makes its opening harmless.
  2. Use the same 70% / -35° player baseline without Dash. Record whether first contact happens before or after a rail boost, then compare energy loss at contact with energy lost while the tops are apart.
  3. If your top exits in Hazard mode, treat that as a route problem rather than an endurance result. Keep Hazard results separate from standard-arena attempts.
  4. Retry with one change: an angle adjustment, or a different power while preserving the angle. Keep several observations because the rival angle and critical check vary.

Base Invader is useful for learning its launch behavior, but a local duel omits its 97 mass / 108 attack Boss configuration. Crown's higher endurance can be a reason to compare low-contact attempts, not evidence that it wins this matchup.

Keep the retry useful

Before Retry, note the stage, both characters, launch settings, arena, first clash, critical or rail effects, and whether the loss was an exit or energy depletion. A loss keeps the stage but resets the current streak; reloading starts stage 1 and clears the current streak. No practice link skips directly to a progression Boss.

For comparable records, also keep the viewport and actual canvas dimensions fixed. See the battle record checklist.
