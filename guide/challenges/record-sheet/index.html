Emoji Blade Matchup Record Sheet

Use one row per round. The purpose is to make a comparison repeatable, not to turn a small sample into a universal ranking.

Record before launching

FieldWrite down
Date and browserYYYY-MM-DD; browser and device
Top pair and modePlayer 1 / Player 2; Standard or Hazard; solo or local 2P
ViewportCSS viewport and canvas size; keep it fixed between repeats
Launch settingsPower and angle for both players
Optional actionsDash, Xtreme rail, or no action

Record after the round

  • First contact and whether it rebounded, entered a pocket, or produced an Xtreme boost.
  • Critical feedback, remaining energy for both tops, and the final result.
  • Any reset, cancellation, resize, or accidental control input.

How to compare rows

Repeat identical settings at least once before changing one angle or power value. Swap left and right when practical. Critical checks are random, and a single round cannot establish a win rate. Keep the notes with the exact URL and commit version if you publish a result.
