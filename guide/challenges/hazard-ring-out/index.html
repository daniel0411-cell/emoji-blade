Hazard Ring-Out: Pocket Safety Test

Learn how to test the left and right Hazard pockets while separating a ring-out observation from a normal wall rebound.

Before you start

Open Hazard mode and keep the viewport fixed. The side pockets can end a round when a top exits at sufficient speed or during Dash or an Xtreme boost; slow contacts rebound.

Repeatable setup

  1. Use a 70% launch, then try a shallow angle toward one side. Watch the top after the first pocket contact. Do not describe the illustrated direction as a guaranteed route: collision speed and timing change the result.
  2. Keep the same viewport and top pair for the first comparison so the result is interpretable.
  3. Write down the launch settings, first contact, critical or Xtreme feedback, and remaining energy.

How to review the result

Repeat with the same top and settings, then change only the angle. Record whether the pocket contact rebounded, exited, or triggered a boost before changing the setup.
