Boss 10 Invader: A Controlled Trial

A controlled way to study the stronger stage 10 Invader without presenting one preset as a guaranteed counter.

Before you start

Reach stage 10 in solo Streak Mode. The boosted Invader has different mass and attack from its base preset, so a local practice link is useful for controls but not a Boss replica.

Repeatable setup

  1. Start with 70% power and -145° for the rival side. Keep both launch settings visible, avoid Dash on the first attempt, and note the first contact, energy drain, and whether a rail boost or critical occurred.
  2. Keep the same viewport and top pair for the first comparison so the result is interpretable.
  3. Write down the launch settings, first contact, critical or Xtreme feedback, and remaining energy.

How to review the result

Repeat the same settings before drawing a conclusion. If you change a variable, change one only. Treat the numbers as an observation record rather than a win-rate claim.
