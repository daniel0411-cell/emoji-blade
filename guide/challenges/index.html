How to use a challenge guide

  1. Keep the top pair, viewport and launch power fixed for the first repeat.
  2. Record first contact, critical or Xtreme feedback, remaining energy and outcome.
  3. Change one variable at a time. Random criticals mean one round cannot establish a ranking.
