How to Play Emoji Blade: Rules, Controls & Physics

Emoji Blade transforms Unicode characters into 3D physics-based spinning tops. The objective is simple: outspin your opponent or knock them out of the ring.

1. The Slingshot Launch System

You have two intuitive methods to control your launch:

  • Arena Touch/Mouse Drag: Press and hold directly on your spinning top inside the circular stadium. Drag backward to aim the glowing trajectory line, and release to shoot!
  • Control Panel Sliders: Fine-tune your Launch Power (30% - 100%) and Launch Angle (-180° to +180°) before hitting the “3, 2, 1, GO SHOOT!” button.

2. Understanding Top Physics & Geometry

Your spinning top is governed by four core metrics:

  • Mass (Weight): Determines momentum resistance. Heavier tops suffer less recoil when hit, making them resilient against ring-outs.
  • Attack: Determines the sharpness and kinetic damage dealt on direct collision.
  • Speed (RPM): Affects rotational frequency. High RPM increases the chance of cutting through defensive armor.
  • Endurance (Stamina): Governs how slowly your top loses energy over time and wall contact.

3. Landing Critical Clashes (1.5x Recoil)

A Critical Clash is triggered when your blade strikes the opponent at high relative velocity while cutting across their rotational axis. It triggers:

  • 1.5x damage to the opponent's spin energy meter;
  • Golden spark shockwaves and severe arena screen shake;
  • High knockback impulse that can pin opponents against arena boundaries.

4. Stage Bosses & 2P Local Mode

Every 5th stage in Streak Mode features a massive Stage Boss (such as 👹 Oni Demon or 👾 Retro Invader) with 135% extra mass and special armor teeth. Aim for their flanks rather than head-on collisions to deplete their spin reserve.
